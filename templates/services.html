Signs your AC needs attention

Don't wait for a full breakdown.

Small warning signs usually show up weeks before a major failure. Catching them early is cheaper and keeps you cool through peak summer.

{{ icon("snow") }}

Weak or warm airflow

Often caused by clogged filters, low refrigerant or a failing fan motor.

{{ icon("bolt") }}

Higher power bills

Dirty coils and worn parts make the compressor run longer for the same cooling.

{{ icon("fan") }}

Strange noises

Rattling, grinding or clicking can point to loose parts, motor bearings or capacitors.

{{ icon("gauge") }}

Water leaks or ice

Blocked drains or low gas pressure can cause dripping or frosting on pipes.

Common questions

Good to know before you book.

Have a question that isn't here? Call or WhatsApp us and we'll answer it directly.

How often should my AC be serviced?

For homes, a full service before summer and a check-up mid-season works well. Commercial units that run long hours usually need servicing every 2–3 months, which an AMC plan covers.

Do you service all AC brands?

Yes. We work on split, window, cassette, ductable, tower, package, VRV/VRF and central systems from all major manufacturers, and can source spares for most models.

Why does my AC keep needing gas?

Refrigerant doesn't get used up. If it's low, there is a leak. We find and repair the leak before recharging, so you aren't paying for refills again and again.

Will I get a quote before work starts?

Always. After inspecting the unit, we explain the problem, the parts involved and the cost. Work only begins once you approve it.

What's included in an AMC?

Scheduled preventive visits, filter and coil cleaning, electrical and gas pressure checks, and priority response for breakdowns. Plans are tailored to the number and type of units on site.
