# AR Cooling Solutions Brand Voice Guidelines

Generated 2026-11-15 from one source: the published website (arcooling.in: about page, service, product and spare-part pages, FAQs, taglines). No call transcripts or separate brand documents were available, so treat this as a working guide, not a finished rulebook.

## Summary

AR Cooling Solutions is a Hyderabad AC and HVAC team that sells, installs, repairs and maintains cooling systems and runs a spare-parts desk. The voice is that of an experienced technician explaining things plainly to a customer: honest, practical, specific about the work, never salesy.

## We are / We are not

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Honest: we say what the equipment actually needs | Pushy, or always selling the costlier option |
| Practical: we describe the work in concrete steps | Vague, or full of marketing adjectives |
| Plain-spoken: everyday words a homeowner understands | Jargon-heavy or showing off |
| Thorough: "fixes that last, not quick patch-ups" | Promising speed or cheapness over quality |
| Local: Hyderabad homes, offices and industries | Generic or pretending to be a national chain |

## Voice (constant)

- Lead with the customer's problem ("AC not cooling like before?"), then what we do about it.
- Name the actual work: jet wash, leak test, vacuuming, correct piping, correctly rated parts.
- Short sentences. One idea each. Active voice. "We" for the business, "you/your" for the customer.
- Calm confidence. No exclamation marks, no ALL CAPS, emoji not needed.

## Message pillars

1. Honest diagnosis and recommendations (repair, part or new system, whichever is right).
2. Workmanship in the details nobody sees.
3. Genuine, correctly rated components matched to the unit.
4. One partner for the whole life of a system: sales, installation, repair, AMC, spare parts.
5. Homes, offices and industries across Hyderabad.

## Terminology

- Use: AC, HVAC, AMC, split / window / cassette / ductable, VRV/VRF, gas refilling, deep cleaning, spare parts, compressor, copper coil, "Call/WhatsApp +91 88013 81405", arcooling.in, #arcooling.
- Prefer: "supply and install" over "deals"; "book a visit" over "hurry"; "correctly rated parts" over "best parts".
- Avoid: best, No. 1, cheapest, lowest price, guaranteed, 100%, instant, same-day, limited offer, hurry.
- Never: prices, discounts, offers, guarantees, response times, statistics or customer names unless the owner supplies them for that post; claims about a brand relationship (dealer, authorised) that the site does not state.

## Examples

Good: "Compressor down? We stock scroll, rotary and reciprocating compressors from Copeland, Danfoss and other brands for technicians, contractors and facility teams."

Avoid: "BEST AC SERVICE IN HYDERABAD!!! Lowest prices guaranteed, book now before the offer ends!"
